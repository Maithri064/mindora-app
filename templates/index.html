Traditional sentiment analysis classifies text along a one-dimensional binary axis (positive vs. negative). Mindora's Bidirectional LSTM model classifies across 6 discrete psychological states (Joy, Sadness, Anger, Fear, Love, and Surprise). This enables distinct wellness interventions—for example, treating Fear with somatic box breathing and Anger with physiological cool-downs.

The model was trained on the CARER emotion benchmark dataset, containing verified multi-class self-reported emotional expressions. Performance was evaluated using Categorical Cross-Entropy, Accuracy (~88-91%), Weighted Precision, Recall, and F1-Scores across all 6 emotion categories.
